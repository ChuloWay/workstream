# WS-POL-003-07 — One complete checker command per phase

- Initiative: WS-POL-003
- Durable disposition: Planned
- Intended merge outcome: one internal typed checker service delegates complete
  pre-submission and post-submission evaluation to their canonical owners,
  without a standalone draft-packet precheck or duplicate evidence writer.

## Intent

Implement the [adopted contract](planning/chunks/WS-POL-003-07-single-checker-service-port.md)
after POL-06B. Keep intake and work evaluation distinct. Preserve exact locked
policy lineage, current authority, atomic evidence and completed-attempt replay.

## Current behavior

Discovery on main `d5dd94d3` establishes:

- `PreparedSubmissionBundlePreparationCommand.prepare` compiles the effective
  plan, seals scratch material, materializes under the existing AUTH transaction,
  then persists ART evidence in a fresh transaction.
- `PreparedBundleMaterializationService` already delegates the complete plan
  through the injected CHECKER processor. Its private request contains a live
  prepared artifact and authorization capability; these cannot become public
  command payloads.
- `_PreSubmitEvidenceRepository.persist` checks replay only after execution.
  Its operation identity includes the prepared generation, which is randomly
  assigned for each new prepared artifact. Existing idempotent evidence writes
  therefore do not establish command retry without re-executing members.
- The convenience `PreparedBundlePreSubmitEvidenceService.execute` requires
  a transaction-free session. It cannot replace production's split transaction
  calls without reconciling authority consumption.
- CHECKERS already exposes `PostSubmissionExecutionPort` and strict request,
  result and current-reference contracts. Production execution deliberately
  remains unavailable pending ARCH-04C/04D.
- The registered `submission-precheck` route still invokes draft JSON feedback
  through `CheckerService.pre_submit_check` and `pre_submit_static_feedback`.
  Its dedicated schemas and tests preserve a superseded intake path.

## Bounded change

### Allowed by the adopted contract

- CHECKERS typed phase interfaces, service and composition; corresponding typed
  ART material/result interfaces; focused tests and current documentation.
- Remove the standalone precheck route, dedicated service/runner functions,
  schemas and obsolete tests. Preserve required behavior in canonical intake
  tests. Trace helpers before deletion: `_load_locked_pre_submit_context` still
  serves durable post-submit consumers, and broader caller-owned Submission
  cleanup belongs to ARCH-02I.

### Not allowed

- No facade-local replay cache, evidence table, second compiler, registry or
  reconstructed ART evidence. No prepared handles in detached payloads.
- No activation, contributor checker endpoints, acceptance, evaluator expansion,
  arbitrary execution or changed AUTH permissions.
- No destructive retained-data cleanup or fabricated historic result details.
  The user explicitly admitted the ART-owned replay prerequisite after discovery.

## Design and decisions

The service must delegate each complete phase to its owner, with composition
injecting the existing implementation. Post-submit production remains unavailable
until its already-planned durable executor and authority land.

Do not weaken the adopted completed-attempt replay requirement. The approved
ART-owned prerequisite must establish a stable logical
attempt and validate/read its canonical completed evidence before executing its
members, under the proper authority and transaction boundaries. Determine the
smallest safe persistence change in that owner's bounded plan. Completed replay
must never mint a replacement upload capability or rebind evidence to a new
scratch generation.

After that prerequisite, compose the two phase commands, remove the obsolete
precheck path, and prove that retry returns the canonical result without another
member invocation. Merely wrapping the current `materialize`/`persist` sequence
or demonstrating a caching fake would not meet this requirement.

## Acceptance criteria

- Exactly two complete typed phase commands; no individual-checker selection.
- Canonical pre plan/evidence and stamped post policy are passed through without
  recompilation, reselection or competing persistence.
- Exact resource, phase, generation and authority mismatches fail closed.
- Completed attempt replay returns canonical evidence without member reruns;
  unfinished recovery follows the owning operation's proven rules.
- Real registered evaluator fixtures prove supported configuration, without
  claiming live post-submit execution or broker transport.
- The obsolete JSON-only precheck route and exclusive implementation are absent.

## Risk and review routing

- Risk class: L1.
- Required reviewers: architecture, security, reuse/dedup, QA/test delta,
  documentation; CI integrity if inventories or test admission change.
- Human review focus: preserve completed replay, current authority and exact
  scratch custody while extending ART; do not add a second evidence writer.

## Evidence

| Claim | Command or proof | Result | Remaining uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existing owner seam | Read ART admission/materialization/evidence, CHECKERS public ports and composition | Existing complete-plan execution and evidence write identified | No facade implementation or execution proof yet |
| Replay feasibility | Trace `materialize` before `persist`, operation identity and prepared generation | Current persistence replay cannot prove no member rerun | ART-owned attempt/recovery design required |
| Obsolete path | Trace registered router to service, runner, schemas and tests | Dedicated draft-feedback path remains reachable | Shared consumers must be retained or migrated deliberately |

Verification after implementation must include real ART execution/member counters,
completed replay, mismatched replay, concurrent delivery, cancellation and rollback;
all-pairs resource/phase denial; real post handler conformance; route absence;
appropriate PostgreSQL checks, hosted full suite and at least 90% affected coverage.
These are planned tests, not claims of completed verification.

## Review findings

Independent plan/architecture/reuse inspection confirmed that the adopted
no-rerun guarantee and prohibition on ART lifecycle changes cannot both be met
by a facade over the current implementation. Preserve the guarantee and resolve
the owner prerequisite rather than silently reducing acceptance criteria.
