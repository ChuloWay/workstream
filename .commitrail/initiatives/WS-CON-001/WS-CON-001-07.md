## Design

CON input is immutable scalar source lineage: project/task/submission,
FinalAcceptance, assignment, contributor, frozen policy version, stabilized
artifact hash and correlation UUID. It has no caller-chosen contribution/award
IDs and no receipt-shaped value purporting to authorize recognition. Existing
SQL source guards validate real stored ancestry. Future shared acceptance owns
actual source authority and TASK terminal-state proof; isolated storage controls
here do not claim those effects or permission.

The caller owns one root AsyncSession transaction and the existing REV lifecycle
fence, acquired before owner locks; it stays open through later effects. No
participant commits or rolls back independently. No current policy selection
or adapter availability is re-evaluated: published/retired frozen definitions
remain due even after retirement or binding suspension.

CON loads its exact frozen accepted-submission rule and immutable definitions,
serializes exact source insertion/replay, generates UUIDv7 IDs only for new rows,
and copies the supplied artifact digest unchanged. The compensation owner
receives strict contribution/definition facts through its public port, writes
all awards and returns exact immutable facts. Unpaid means zero; compensated
means one or two exact definitions. Exact replay recovers original IDs and
requires the complete stored award set; conflicts never backfill partial rows.
Returned facts supply later audit/outbox composition; this chunk does not stage
or claim authorized audit/outbox events.

A deferred SQL constraint trigger verifies complete accepted-submission award
membership at commit, allowing the contribution and awards to be staged in
sequence. It also protects late inserts from changing a completed set. All
protected SQL references are schema-qualified and functions pin safe search_path.
Upgrade refuses incomplete retained accepted-submission rows unchanged. Reviewer
completeness remains with its future participant; existing reviewer storage
proof is retained.
